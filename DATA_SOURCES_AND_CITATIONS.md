## 2. Synthetic Fault-Injection Telemetry Benchmark Suite

All Varian TrueBeam `.bin` trajectory logs (header v3.0 / v4.0 / v5.0, 20 ms / 50 Hz sampling, 120-leaf Bank A/B Millennium/HD120 MLC geometry) and 30-day multi-linac TG-142/MPC telemetry drift timelines generated by `linac-forensics` are **controlled synthetic physics benchmarks** constructed with exact ground-truth fault injections (MLC leaf viscous drag $\tau_i$, systematic DLG offset $\Delta\mathrm{gap}$, gantry gravity-sag harmonic $A\sin\theta + B\cos\theta$, ion-chamber MU drift $\delta$) to verify causal recovery accuracy, round-trip binary fidelity, and zero-error mathematical invariants.

Every synthetic record carries an explicit provenance tag (`SYNTHETIC_FAULT_INJECTION_BENCHMARK` or `SYNTHETIC_TG142_FLEET_DRIFT`) in its `ProvenanceRecord` so real OpenKBP anatomy and synthetic linac telemetry are never conflated.
